## Cultural Liquidation and Civil Change Under Occupation

### Main Correction

A freedom policy is not realistic if it only protects services and ignores cultural survival.

Under occupation, the mandate must include:

```text
life protection
+ essential services
+ cultural survival
+ language continuity
+ heritage protection
+ institutional memory
+ protection from forced assimilation
The enemy is not a people.
The enemy is the policy posture of coercive cultural erasure.

Civil Change Under Occupation
Not all change is illegitimate. Some changes are administrative or emergency-based.

A civil change is legitimate only if it passes this test:

legitimate_civil_change:
  required_conditions:
    - protects_civilians
    - is_non_discriminatory
    - preserves_basic_rights
    - is_transparent
    - is_proportionate
    - does_not_erase_identity
    - does_not_force_assimilation
    - preserves_essential_services
    - allows_local_institutional_continuity
A civil change becomes illegitimate occupation policy when it includes:

illegitimate_civil_change:
  indicators:
    - forced_language_replacement
    - banning_local_names
    - destroying_cultural_sites
    - removing_children_from_cultural_education
    - censoring_local_history
    - replacing_local_law_without_local_consent
    - collective_punishment
    - forced_displacement
    - targeting_religious_or_cultural_practice
    - erasing_local_institutions
